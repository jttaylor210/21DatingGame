No-Bullshit 21

Twenty-one questions. You pick how deep you go. They decide whether to believe you.

Rounds
House rules
  1. Take turns. The answerer picks the stakes: Surface is worth 1, Under is 2, Core is 3. The asker draws the question and reads it out loud.
  2. Answer out loud. The phone is the deck and the scoreboard. Nothing else goes through it.
  3. The asker rules: Real, or Bullshit. Bullshit doesn't mean "you're lying." It means "that was the brochure answer. Give me the real one."
  4. Called? Go again. Come clean and you still get full points. Stay slippery and you get nothing, and the asker gets one.
  5. Two passes each. No explanation owed. The pass is an answer too.
  6. The asker's ruling is final. You're judging each other all night, so rule the way you'd want to be ruled.
